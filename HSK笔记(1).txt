三、语法 Grammar
1. S + 会 + V + 吗？ ｜ S + 会 + V / S + 不会 + V
Meaning: Can subject do something? Subject can / cannot do something.

- 你会说汉语吗？
Nǐ huì shuō Hànyǔ ma?
Can you speak Chinese?
- 我会说汉语。
Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ.
I can speak Chinese.
- 我不会说汉语。
Wǒ bú huì shuō Hànyǔ.
I cannot speak Chinese.

2. 怎么 + V  how + verb
① sth + 怎么 + V 
eg. 这个字怎么写？
Zhège zì zěnme xiě?
How do you write this character?
② 怎么 + V + sth 
eg. 怎么读这个字？
Zěnme dú zhège zì?
How do you read this character?

三、语法 Grammar
1. 名词谓语句 Noun‑predicate sentence
结构：S + 名词（年龄、日期、国籍、名字等） 
Structure: S + Noun (age, date, nationality, name, etc.)
不需要动词 “是” No verb “是 (shì)” is needed 
例句： 
我二十岁。 Wǒ èrshí suì. I am twenty years old. 
我中国人。 Wǒ Zhōngguó rén. I am Chinese.

2. S + 去 (+ 地点) + 动词短语
Structure: Subject + qù (+place) + verb phrase 
含义：（主语）去（某地）做……

# 四、语法 Grammar
1. 量词：个(gè) 和 口(kǒu) 的区别
个 gè：the most commonly used measure word.
If you cannot remember a specific measure word, you may use “gè”.
口 kǒu：mainly for family members (家里几口人)
例句：你家有几口人？ How many people are there in your family?

2. 疑问代词：多少 duōshao
“多少” is used to ask numbers. The measure word can be omitted.
“多少钱”：ask price
结构：多少 +（量词）+ 名词
例：多少（个）学生？ How many students?

会

  二、语法（English explanation + 例句）

  1. 太…… 了

 Grammar explanation : Used to express an extreme degree, usually with subjective feeling (good or bad). Structure: 太 + Adjective + 了

- 太贵了｜tài guì le｜too expensive
- 太帅了｜tài shuài le｜so handsome
- 太美了｜tài měi le｜so beautiful
- 太难了｜tài nán le｜too difficult

  2. Degree words: 有点儿 / 不太

 Grammar explanation : 有点儿 + adj = a little…; 不太 + adj = not very…

- 很热｜hěn rè｜very hot
- 不太热｜bù tài rè｜not very hot
- 有点儿热｜yǒu diǎnr rè｜a little hot
- 太热了｜tài rè le｜too hot
- 很冷｜hěn lěng｜very cold
- 不太冷｜bù tài lěng｜not very cold
- 有点儿冷｜yǒu diǎnr lěng｜a little cold
- 太冷了｜tài lěng le｜too cold
- 不冷不热｜bù lěng bù rè｜neither cold nor hot

  3. 怎么 + Verb

 Grammar explanation : Ask the method of doing an action. Structure: 怎么 + Verb

- 怎么写｜zěn me xiě｜how to write
- 怎么读｜zěn me dú｜how to read
- 怎么说｜zěn me shuō｜how to say

  4. Noun / Pronoun + 怎么样

 Grammar explanation : Ask about condition, quality or feeling of someone / something.

- 天气怎么样？｜tiān qì zěn me yàng？｜How is the weather?
- 他怎么样？｜tā zěn me yàng？｜How is he?
- 他很好。｜tā hěn hǎo。｜He is very well.
- 你今天怎么样？｜nǐ jīn tiān zěn me yàng？｜How are you today?
- 我今天很好。｜wǒ jīn tiān hěn hǎo。｜I’m fine today.

  5. 会 (huì)
① can: master an ability through learning
② will: express possibility or plan in the future
Structure: Subject + modal verb (会) + Verb (+ Object) + 吗？(question)

- 今天你会来学校吗？｜jīn tiān nǐ huì lái xué xiào ma？｜Will you come to school today?
- 你今天会来学校吗？｜nǐ jīn tiān huì lái xué xiào ma？｜Will you come to school today?

  6. 多 / 少 + Verb

多 + Verb = do more of the action；
少 + Verb = do less of the action

- 多喝水｜duō hē shuǐ｜drink more water
- 多吃饭｜duō chī fàn｜eat more
- 多吃些水果｜duō chī xiē shuǐ guǒ｜eat more fruit
- 少吃蛋糕｜shǎo chī dàn gāo｜eat less cake
- 少吃些薯片｜shǎo chī xiē shǔ piàn｜eat fewer potato chips

  7. 多少

 Grammar explanation : Ask quantity, followed by nouns.

- 多少钱？｜duō shao qián？｜How much money?
- 多少个学生？｜duō shao gè xué sheng？｜How many students?
- 多少岁？｜duō shao suì？｜How old?

一、词汇
喂：wèi｜hello (used on the phone) 也：yě｜also; too 学习：xué xí｜study; learn 上午：shàng wǔ｜morning 睡觉：shuì jiào｜sleep 电视：diàn shì｜TV; television 喜欢：xǐ huān｜like; be fond of 给…… 打电话：gěi … dǎ diàn huà｜call sb; make a phone call to somebody
二、语法（English explanation）
1. 喂 wèi
Grammar explanation: “喂” is used only at the start of a phone call to attract attention, equal to “hello” in phone conversations.
例句：喂？请问是李老师吗？｜wèi？qǐng wèn shì Lǐ lǎo shī ma？｜Hello? Is this Teacher Li?
2. 在…… 呢 zài … ne (present continuous)
Grammar explanation: This structure shows an action is in progress now. Structure: Subject + 在 + Verb + (Object) + （呢）
例句：我在看书呢。｜wǒ zài kàn shū ne。｜I am reading a book.
Negative form：Subject + 没（在）+ Verb + (Object) + （呢）
例句：我没在看书。

3. 电话号码 diàn huà hào mǎ
Grammar explanation: Chinese people read telephone numbers one digit by one digit.
例句：我的电话号码是 18230415566。｜wǒ de diàn huà hào mǎ shì 82304155。｜My phone number is 18230415566.
注：1 in phone number read “yāo”
4. 吧 ba (imperative sentence ending)
Grammar explanation: Put at the end of imperative sentences for suggestion or mild command, soften the tone.
例句：你下午打吧。｜nǐ xià wǔ dǎ ba。｜You can call her in the afternoon.
